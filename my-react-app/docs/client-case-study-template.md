# Adding an approved client case study

No real case studies are currently supplied. Keep existing illustrative entries labelled as concepts until verified project material is available.

For each future case study, collect the approved project/client name, permission to publish, the business problem, J4's specific role, what was delivered, approved screenshots with descriptive alt text, a live/demo link if public, and any measured results with their source and time period. Omit results that cannot be verified. Obtain permission before publishing a client testimonial or attribution.

The public case study can then show the problem, approach, deliverables and verified outcome. Its status should distinguish a concept, a prototype and a delivered product.
